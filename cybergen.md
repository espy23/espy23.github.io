## CyberGEN.IQ Aptitude Assessment
I was fortunate enough to take the CyberGEN.IQ aptitude and talent assessment by Haystack Solutions as part of the WiCyS Mentorship Program. It helped me better understand my problem-solving style and natural technical abilities.

The assessment evaluates cognitive constructs, specifically Exhaustive analysis, Critical Thinking, Initiating, Real-Time processing, and Responding, and maps them across four primary cybersecurity operational quadrants: Offensive Operations, Defensive Operations, Design & Development, and Analysis & Forensics.

### My Results
My overall assessment profile highlights strong capabilities in deep analytical problem-solving, sustained mental focus, and complex pattern recognition.

#### Quadrant Scores
- Design & Development: 71 / 100
- Offensive Operations: 66 / 100
- Analysis & Forensics: 66 / 100
- Defensive Operations: 61 / 100

> Note on scoring:
> * 0-35 = less likely to excel
> * 35-50 = likely to excel
> * 50-65 = strong aptitude
> * 65+ = very strong aptitude

#### Standout Metrics
- Mental Model Ability: Demonstrates an advanced capacity to build complex mental models and rapidly test hypotheses when surveying networks or investigating anomalous behavior.
- Pattern Vigilance: Measures the ability to maintain deep concentration for long periods of time. Useful for monitoring large volumes of security alerts and understanding which matter without being lulled to boredom by those that don’t.
- Visuospatial Working Memory: Highlights the ability to focus in the midst of distractions and a strong working memory capable of juggling multiple factors simultaneously.
- Creative Thinking: Reflects a free-flowing, creative approach to connecting seemingly unrelated data points to uncover indicators of attack.
- Exhaustive Problem Solving: Shows a persistent drive to push through technical obstacles and follow chains of evidence all the way to root cause resolution.
